A simple workflow you can repeat for every event.

Use the same structure whether you are managing five vendors or fifty.

Capture

Record what each vendor has agreed to provide, the exact deliverable, and the person responsible.

Date

Attach the promised date, payment milestone, confirmation date, or event-day deadline.

Track

Mark each commitment by status so you can see what is complete, pending, late, or blocked.

Follow up

Reach out only when the status or due date says you need to — not because you are worried you forgot something.
